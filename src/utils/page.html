Reheat Instructions

Here are some general tips to ensure our meals taste their best. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out at any time.

Seafood

Skip the microwave (it can make things rubbery). Instead:

  • Best: Quick pan-fry with a little oil.
  • Also great: Cover with foil and warm at 375°F in the oven.

Proteins & veggies by the pound

  • Most items: Cover with foil and warm in the oven to keep moisture in.
  • Want a more crispy finish? Uncover for the last few minutes.
  • Panko chicken: 375°F for 15-18 minutes until heated through

Ready-made meals

Mixed meals (like our Sesame Wheat Noodles): Quick saute with a splash of neutral oil.

Multi-item meals (like Mojo Chicken):

  1. Peel back film.
  2. Remove sauce.
  3. Microwave 2-2:30 minutes with film loosely on.
  4. Add sauce after heating.

Our Packaging

Our packaging is all made made from plant-based, recycled, biodegradable plastic that's BPA free.

We don't use lids to avoid waste. Instead, we use a plant-based film provides an air and watertight seal to keep food fresh for up to 5 days in the fridge.

The containers are microwaveable, freezable, and washable.
